# Internal notes

Staff only. Nothing in this folder is ever built or published — `scripts/build-all.mjs` scans
`clients/` and nothing else, and `npm run verify` checks that no page from here appears in any
client's output.

- [access-rules.md](access-rules.md) — who can open which client's docs, and the wildcard trap
- [runbook.md](runbook.md) — what to do when something goes wrong

Put anything here that clients must not see: commercial terms, internal estimates, notes about a
client rather than for them.
